# Story Expansion Plan (Phase 0, planning only)

Acts 1 and 2 are preserved exactly (Arin, the three seals, the Sun Gate, the Lantern Trail, the shards, the lost cow, the letter, Night of the Reavers, the Bandit Camp, Captain Karan). Everything below is **proposed and needs your approval** before it is written. Names marked (new) are suggestions.

## Cast
| Character | Role | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| Arin | Protagonist, archaeologist's heir | From curious scholar to keeper of the Sun Gate; her choices shape the ending |
| Captain Karan | Reaver leader (Act 2 boss) | Act 3 reveals he was a royal guard who lost his oath, not a plain bandit |
| Elder Kamla | Village elder, Water-Seal keeper | Guides Arin; her sealed well is tied to the underground waterway |
| Hari, Anita, Weaver, Merchant | Existing villagers | Rebuilding and personal side quests |
| Tara | Prisoner rescued in the Reaver Fort (already planned) | First companion: a scout who knows the roads |
| Ilan (new) | Mysterious Act 3 companion | Last descendant of the royal archivists; secretly bound to the ancient guardians |
| Queen Ila (historical) | The sealer of the kingdom | Revealed through royal records |
| Vessa (new) | Act 4 antagonist, warlord of the Ashen Host | Seeks the Sun Gate's power to end the drought by force |

## Act 3: The Kingdom Beneath
Locations: a hidden stair behind the Sun Gate; the Underground City of Veyra; the Archive Hall; the Guardian Gallery; the Drowned Cistern.
Main quests: (1) Descend: the gate opens to a stair; (2) The Silent Streets: explore, restore lights; (3) The Royal Records: three tablets reveal Ila's choice; (4) Ilan joins; (5) The Guardians: puzzle trials (pressure plates, light reflection); (6) Karan's Oath: truth about Karan; (7) Boss: the Stone Warden.
Side quests: the Lamp-Lighter's Debt; the Lost Choir; the Cistern Fish (ties to fishing).
Decisions: spare or condemn Karan; free or bind the guardians.
Boss: **Stone Warden** (multi-phase, puzzle-assisted).

## Act 4: The Fallen Crown
New faction: the Ashen Host, led by Vessa. Kingdom-wide conflict reaches the village.
Main quests: (1) The Burning Road; (2) Betrayal: a trusted ally (proposed: the Merchant or Ilan, depending on earlier choices) hands the seals to Vessa; (3) Captured companions; rescue missions at an outpost and a fortress; (4) Alliances: the Village, the Caravan guild, and (if spared) Karan's men; (5) The Siege of the village; (6) Boss: **Vessa**.
Side quests: supply lines, bounty targets, escort the caravan.
Decisions: which allies to rescue first; who to trust; whether to burn the supply road.

## Act 5: The Last Sun
Truth: the Sun Gate does not hold a treasure; it is the valve that releases the stored water of the kingdom, at the cost of the Gate's keeper.
Main quests: (1) The Final Road; (2) Ancient Revelations; (3) Final dungeon: the Solar Vault; (4) Multi-stage boss: **the Eclipse Guardian**; (5) The final choice.
Endings (depend on tracked choices):
1. **Heroic**: Arin and her allies open the Gate together; the valley flourishes (requires most allies alive and the village rebuilt).
2. **Sacrifice**: Arin becomes the Gate's keeper to save everyone (requires deep trust with a companion).
3. **Secret**: Karan's redeemed oath and Queen Ila's records unlock a way to open the Gate with no sacrifice (requires spared Karan, all records, all seals).

## Tracked choices (stored in the `GameState`)
karan_fate, guardians_bound, ally_betrayer, allies_rescued, village_rebuild_level, trust_tara, trust_ilan, records_found, seals_kept.

## Pacing and quality rules
No repeating fetch quests; every side quest must change something (a shop, a route, an NPC line). Each act ends with a cinematic finale using the in-game world.
