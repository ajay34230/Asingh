## Music and synthesised sounds

Original, generated by `tools/make_music.py` and `tools/make_sfx.py`.

## Voices

Spoken Hindi dialogue generated with **Kokoro-82M** (hexgrad, Apache-2.0 open weights,
huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) by `tools/make_voice.py`. Voices: hm_omega (announcer),
hm_psi, hf_alpha, hf_beta. Each line was checked for intelligibility by transcribing it
with Whisper.
