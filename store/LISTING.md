## Full description (max 4000)
The Lost Kingdom is an offline 3D adventure set in a sun-baked desert land. Arin has vanished, and the only clues are scattered across villages, bazaars, farms and ruins.

* Explore a large open world on foot, by horse, donkey or bull, or in a cart race.
* Fight with boxing and kicking moves: jab, cross, hook, uppercut, and front, round and side kicks. Dodge, roll, block and lock on to enemies.
* Take on bounties from the bounty board and earn coins.
* Run your own farm: plant, water, harvest and sell crops at the market. Rain helps your fields.
* Meet villagers, trade at stalls, follow story missions and read your journal.
* Day and night, weather and festivals change the world around you.
* Hindi and English text.
* Touch controls built for phones. Graphics adapt to your device.
* No ads. No in-app purchases. No internet needed. No data collected.

This is an early version of the game. Please report bugs by email.
